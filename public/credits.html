✦ BLUFF MASTERS
THE FACTS BEHIND THE BLUFFS

Question credits

2,060 questions. Ten categories. One Potluck.

The bank includes the original 30 Bluff Masters questions, 1,547 adapted questions from Open Trivia Database, 424 adapted questions from OpenTriviaQA, and 59 newly worded Bluff Masters questions, including 30 researched Weird & Wacky additions.

Open Trivia Database is contributed by its community and provided by PixelTail Games; its data was obtained from leakyhose's archive. OpenTriviaQA is collected and maintained by uberspot and its contributors.

The community questions, their adaptations, and the 59 newly worded questions are available under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International. Selection, text decoding, category assignment, wording corrections, and some answer/decoy edits were made for this game. No endorsement by the original contributors is implied.

The new oddities use original wording informed by Ig Nobel research, Natural History Museum, NASA, Smithsonian, National Geographic and Guinness World Records. Individual research links are recorded in the repository question bank.

Source details and the license are included in the repository's question credits.
